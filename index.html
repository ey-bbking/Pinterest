Privacy Policy - Deals for Women

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Deals for Women ("we") operates a personal tool that publishes pins to our own Pinterest account and boards.

What the app does

The app creates pins with product images and descriptions on our own boards. It reads only our own boards (to get a board ID) and does not access, collect, or store data about any other Pinterest user.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data from visitors or other Pinterest users. The app stores an access token for our own account on a private server, used only to publish our pins.

Affiliate disclosure

Pins may contain affiliate links. We may earn a commission on purchases made through them, at no extra cost to you.

Third parties

We do not sell or share personal data. Links in our pins lead to third-party stores that have their own privacy policies.
